# 🎮 Global Video Game Market Analysis: From Data to Strategy

## 📋 Project Overview
This project performs a comprehensive analysis of the video game market using **Python**. Following the Google Data Analytics methodology, the study transitions from raw data cleaning to the identification of niche regional behaviors and strategic catalog segmentation using the Pareto principle.

Despite being developed entirely within the Python ecosystem, the depth of analysis allows for the identification of key partners and "star products" for business decision-making.

---

## 🧠 Methodology (Google Data Analytics Framework)
The analysis was structured following the six phases of the Google methodology to ensure actionable results:

1. **Ask:** Definition of business objectives, focused on understanding regional profitability and catalog performance.
2. **Prepare:** Collection and validation of global historical sales datasets.
3. **Process:** Deep data cleaning with Python, addressing null values, duplicates, and variable normalization.
4. **Analyze:** Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and application of **ABC/Pareto Classification**.
5. **Visualize:** Communication of findings through trend charts and market share visualizations.
6. **Act:** Translation of findings into strategic recommendations for marketing and logistics.

---

## 🚀 Key Findings & Insights
* **ABC Segmentation (Pareto):** Identification of "Star Video Games" (Category A) representing 80% of the business value, optimizing the focus on inventory management.
* **Regional Analysis (The Japan Case):** An atypical consumption behavior was detected in the Japanese market, with a dominant preference for niche genres that do not hold the same relevance in Western markets (NA/EU).
* **Key Partners:** Determination of strategic publishers based on their sales volume and critical success within the catalog.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technical Skills
* **Language:** Python
* **Data Cleaning:** Advanced use of `Pandas` (handling `missing values`, `pivot_tables`, `merge`, and `lambda functions`).
* **Strategic Analysis:** Logical implementation of the Pareto Principle (80/20) for product categorization.
* **Visualization:** `Matplotlib` and `Seaborn` to represent regional distributions and the sales concentration curve.

---

## 💡 Strategic Conclusions
* **Regional Personalization:** Proposal for specific marketing campaigns for Japan based on its unique genre preferences.
* **Inventory Efficiency:** Recommendations for Class C title turnover and stock prioritization for Class A titles.
